I was classically trained from ages 4 through 14. Around 12, I discovered Costantino Carrara's piano covers on YouTube, which inspired me to start messing around with pop covers. Over the next few years I found that adding my own flourishes and creative touches came more naturally when learning by ear, and the songs required no additional effort to memorize.

My early covers were learned with a combination of sheet music and by ear, but that balance has shifted more and more toward ear, often kick-started with guitar tabs to learn the chords.

My covers are recorded in two parts. The audio is recorded first, using a Casio CDP-S350 keyboard wired through Logic Pro X. I use a digital piano sound from Garritan called CFX Lite, which models the Yamaha CFX Grand piano. The video is filmed separately using multiple shots.

One cover has reached 60K+ views. Free tutorials and sheet music are available for a handful of songs — and for others, I'm happy to share MIDI files on request.
