Despite being home to the Institute for Quantum Computing, Waterloo had no quantum computing student club. After attending Quantum Days 2025 in Toronto and seeing what other universities were building, I came back and co-founded one with Chaitanya Sharma, Prachee Nanda, Iman Umair-Qaiser, and Lavanya Yadav.

We launched in the summer term with weekly lectures and events, averaging 10–15 attendees per week. By fall, with Juman Amro leading marketing, attendance grew substantially. The club now runs independently with its own executive team.
