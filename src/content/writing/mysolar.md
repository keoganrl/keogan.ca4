At mySolar, I wore many hats. My days consisted mostly of glorified IT work. The central CRM system the company ran off of was a work in progress, so I was in weekly communication with the CEO of that company (Sunbase). I created custom email templates to customers using HTML and CSS, which I had learned only weeks prior while making this website. I managed several requests per day, every day, to keep things running smoothly for everyone from customers to coworkers behind the scenes.

I learned the ins and outs of residential solar operations. We did not manufacture the panels, so most of the technical knowledge I acquired was business-related rather than science-related.

I ended up at mySolar through a connection made the previous summer — Brendan, the CEO of the pest control company I'd done door-to-door sales for, had become a co-owner of mySolar and referred me directly. It was a wonderful change of pace from submitting 700 applications the term before.
